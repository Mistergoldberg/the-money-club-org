Built for learning — not profit

The Money Club.Org is a community-first program.

It is not designed to maximize profit. It is designed to teach financial literacy by example.

That's why we run the program using open-book financials — so families can see exactly where tuition goes, and students can learn by watching money move through a real system.

How It Works

During the program, students will:

  • Create a product or service
  • Learn basic business math (costs, pricing, margin)
  • Work in teams
  • Practice communication and leadership
  • Prepare and present at a community pitch night

By the end, every student can confidently explain what they built, how it works, and why it matters.

The Flywheel

How the system sustains itself
  • Parents fund the program
  • Students operate real projects
  • Tuition recirculates into materials and instruction
  • Learning compounds each cohort

Curriculum

  • Financial Literacy as Survival Gear Understand gross margin, cash flow, sourcing, and unit economics.
  • Sourcing 101: Where Value Is Actually Made How products are sourced, priced, bundled, and scaled.
  • Market Research: Follow the Money & Problems Find real demand signals and paid problems.
  • Control Brand Playbook Learn how private labels create pricing power.
  • Design Thinking & Human Factor Research Observe behavior and turn friction into opportunities.
  • Cambridge Business Model Canvas Turn insight into a real business model.
  • Go-To-Market: Blast Off Without Burning Fuel Launch fast, test cheaply, and avoid premature scaling.
  • Creative Fundamentals Simple design, photography, and visual clarity that raise perceived value.
  • Marketing: Ride Existing Channels Use marketplaces and partners before building your own platform.
  • Always-On Market Research Track competitors, pricing shifts, and trends.
