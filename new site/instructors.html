Meaningful Employment

The Money Club.Org is a paid summer opportunity for university students to run a real, community-based entrepreneurship program.

You'll help young people build and sell real products, manage real budgets, and make real decisions while gaining hands-on experience in economics, public policy, education, and social impact.

This isn't tutoring and it's not volunteering. It's operational leadership in a supervised, closed-loop system with pay, responsibility, and measurable outcomes.

Who This Is For

This opportunity is well-suited for students studying or interested in:
  • Economics
  • Public policy
  • Political science
  • Non-profit management
  • Education
  • Urban studies

  • Business, marketing, or entrepreneurship
