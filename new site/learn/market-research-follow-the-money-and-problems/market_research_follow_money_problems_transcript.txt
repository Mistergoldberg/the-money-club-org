product assortment analysis means studying what products a store carries to understand how a category works
a sku is one specific product listing
a forty five litre garbage bag from glad is one sku
a ninety litre recycling bag from great value is another sku
a ten litre compostable liner is another sku
when a store has many skus in one size or type that can be a demand signal
it may mean many customers buy that kind of product
it may mean suppliers are competing there
it may mean the retailer believes that shelf space is worth it
not proof
a clue
good market research collects clues before making decisions
