the coffee system
why a cup of coffee teaches more about business than a lemonade stand
everybody knows the lemonade stand
buy lemons
add sugar
make a sign
sell a cup
somebody gives you a dollar
business feels simple
that is why adults love using lemonade stands to teach entrepreneurship
but lemonade stands hide something important
they make selling look like the whole game
and selling is not the whole game
if you want to understand how money actually moves stop looking at lemonade
look at coffee
coffee is the grown-up lemonade stand
same basic idea
a drink
a customer
a price
a transaction
but one becomes a summer project
the other becomes a billion-dollar industry
why?
because coffee is not just a product
coffee is a system
and systems are where the real lessons live
imagine you walk into a coffee shop and buy a coffee for two dollars
simple
you hand over two dollars
the store gives you a coffee
transaction complete
except that is not what actually happened
that is only the part you can see
underneath that coffee is an entire machine working together
coffee beans
milk
sugar
cups
lids
machines
electricity
staff
rent
cleaning
delivery trucks
payment systems
marketing
loyalty programs
apps
managers
suppliers
someone had to decide the price
someone had to design the menu
someone had to make sure the store opened on time
someone had to predict how much coffee people would buy today
the cup is visible
the system is invisible
most people only see the cup
business people learn to see the system
and once you start seeing systems you begin noticing them everywhere
schools are systems
hospitals are systems
airports are systems
cities are systems
businesses are systems
the students who learn to see systems early gain an advantage
because they stop asking:
what product should i make?
and start asking:
what system creates this result?
that question is the beginning of business thinking
and it starts with a cup of coffee
